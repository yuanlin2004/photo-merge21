# Silver Jubilee demo photographs

AI-generated fictional family keepsakes, not archival photographs. The historical setting is a London street party on June 7, 1977, during Queen Elizabeth II's Silver Jubilee.

- `front.png`: grandmother and granddaughter Annie at a neighborhood street-party table.
- `back.png`: the imagined reverse of the same print, with June's handwritten note.
- `flip-demo.gif`: looping rendered animation using the viewer’s perspective geometry and easing, with pauses on each side. Recreate it with `python3 scripts/render-demo.py` from the repository root (requires Pillow and NumPy).
- `merged.png`: both scans joined horizontally, with metadata for Photo Flip.

Fictional handwritten note:

> Jubilee Day — 7 June 1977<br>
> Mum and little Annie outside our house.<br>
> Everyone brought a chair and something<br>
> for the table. Mum’s sponge went first!<br>
> Annie wore her crown home to bed.<br>
> — June

Created using the built-in image-generation tool. Full prompts are retained in `prompts.txt`.

Historical context: [London Museum](https://www.londonmuseum.org.uk/collections/london-stories/pageantry-protest-elizabeth-silver-jubilee/).
